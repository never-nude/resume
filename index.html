About

About Mike.

02 / READ

I'm a mixed-media artist, and I build software the same way I make a sculpture — start with something abstract and shape it into a thing you can actually use.

The work runs a wide range — a civic tool a real council depends on, a museum you walk through online, apps that scan a house or recover a lost signal. I build all of it myself, from the first idea to the version people actually use.

It shows most in how I use AI. On the civic tool, the AI advises and a person decides — every call is logged, every judgment is reviewable, and nothing happens automatically behind the scenes. I'd rather build something people can trust than something they have to take on faith.
